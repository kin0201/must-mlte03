The Digital Lab

數字教學實驗室 — 每個概念都能看見、能撥動、能親手試

Twenty-five small instruments, one per idea the course is built on. Each runs in this page with no install: drag the sliders, press the buttons, watch the plot recompute. Every card ends with the quadsim command that turns the same idea into a flight you can run yourself. Use them before the lecture to predict, during it to check, after it to test the edge cases nobody had time for.
